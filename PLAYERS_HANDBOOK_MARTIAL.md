## 1. THE FIGHTER (WARRIOR GROUP)

The **Fighter** is the definitive master of weapons, battlefield tactics, and frontline martial prowess. In AD&D 2nd Edition, the Fighter stands as the backbone of every adventuring party, boasting the highest hit points, the heaviest armor allowances, the fastest attack bonus progression, and exclusive access to **Weapon Specialization**.

### Class Profile & Archetype Metrics
* **Class Group:** Warrior
* **Primary Requisite:** Strength (16+)
* **Hit Die:** 1d10 per level (Starting HP: 12 + Constitution Modifier)
* **Base Armor Class:** AC 3 (Banded Mail & Medium Shield)
* **Starting Base Attack Bonus:** +2 to-hit
* **Attack Growth:** +1.0 to-hit every level unconditionally (Best in the game)
* **Default Starting Weapon:** Longsword
* **Allowed Equipment:** All armors, all shields, all melee weapons, and all missile weapons.

### Standard Ability Array
| Attribute | Score | Key Effect |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Strength** | 16 | +1 Damage, +35 lbs Carry Capacity, +2 Door Bash |
| **Dexterity** | 12 | Standard Reaction & Missile Modifier |
| **Constitution** | 15 | +1 HP per Hit Die (+4 at 18 Con for Warriors) |
| **Intelligence** | 9 | Basic Literacy & Tactic Comprehension |
| **Wisdom** | 10 | Standard Mental Fortitude |
| **Charisma** | 11 | Normal Henchmen & Morale Modifiers |

### Innate Fighter Skills
* **Bash Door:** Base score = `Strength + 2 + (Level - 1)`. Used to smash through barred heavy timber doors, iron grates, and stuck dungeon gates.
* **Intimidate:** Base score = `Strength + 2 + (Level - 1)`. Used during parleys and hostile encounters to force enemy morale checks and demand surrenders.

## 2. WEAPON SPECIALIZATION & WEAPON MASTERY

In AD&D 2nd Edition rules, **Weapon Specialization is strictly exclusive to the Fighter class**. While other classes are limited to basic weapon proficiency, Fighters dedicate endless hours to perfecting their craft with a single chosen weapon.

### Weapon Specialization Bonus
When wielding their designated specialized weapon:
* **To-Hit Bonus:** **+1 bonus to attack rolls**
* **Damage Bonus:** **+2 bonus to damage rolls**
* **Specialization Re-Assignment:** At each level-up advancement, a Fighter may choose to affirm or change their specialized weapon with their training mentor.

### Available Weapon Specialization Arsenal
1. **Longsword** (1d8 Slashing, 1-Handed) — Classic knightly blade with balanced reach and speed.
2. **Two-Handed Sword** (1d10 Slashing, 2-Handed) — Devastating sweep attacks that sacrifice shield defense for maximum damage.
3. **Warhammer** (1d4+1 Bludgeoning, 1-Handed) — Crushing armor-piercing weapon effective against skeletons and heavy plate.
4. **Short Sword** (1d6 Piercing, 1-Handed) — Quick thrusting blade ideal for close-quarter hallway engagements.
5. **Mace** (1d6 Bludgeoning, 1-Handed) — Heavy iron flanged striker.
6. **Halberd** (1d10 Slashing/Piercing, 2-Handed) — Long polearm with severe reach advantage.
7. **Quarterstaff** (1d6 Bludgeoning, 2-Handed) — Sturdy ash wood combat staff.
8. **Short Bow** (1d6 Piercing, 2-Handed Ranged) — Fast woodland hunting bow.
9. **Long Bow** (1d8 Piercing, 2-Handed Ranged) — High-draw military warbow with extended distance.
10. **Crossbow** (1d8 Piercing, 2-Handed Ranged) — Heavy mechanical arbalest with high kinetic penetration.
11. **Sling** (1d4 Bludgeoning, 1-Handed Ranged) — Lightweight bullet sling with silent release.

### Dynamic Weapon Mastery Tiers (Usage Progression)
As heroes engage in real combat, repeated strikes and victories unlock permanent mastery tiers:

| Mastery Tier | Minimum Level | Successful Hits Required | Granted Combat Bonuses |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Unranked** | Level 1 | 0 | Standard weapon proficiency |
| **Familiarity** | Level 2 | 15 Hits | **+1 To-Hit** (Stacks with Specialization) |
| **Mastery** | Level 5 | 40 Hits | **+2 To-Hit, +1 Damage** (Stacks with Specialization) |

*A Level 5 Fighter specialized in the Longsword with Mastery Tier achieved gains a total of **+3 To-Hit and +3 Damage** on every strike!*

### Thief (Rogue) Detailed Progression
* **Hit Die:** 1d6 (Max Con HP bonus is capped at +2 for non-warriors).
* **Attack Bonus Rate:** +0.5 per level (+1 every 2 levels).
* **Discretionary Skill Points:** **+15 Points per level** to freely allocate among thieving skills.

#### Base Thief Skills (Percentile d100):
* **Pick Locks:** Base 25% (+Dex adjustment)
* **Find/Disarm Traps:** Base 20% (+Dex adjustment)
* **Pick Pockets:** Base 30% (+Dex adjustment)
* **Hide in Shadows:** Base 15% (+Dex adjustment, +25% with Boots of Elvenkind)
* **Hear Noise:** Base 20%

#### Specialized Rogue Progression Tracks:
* **Shadow Track (Stealth / Scout):**
  * *Familiarity (Lvl 3, 10 Sneaks):* Reduces party size penalty on Sneak Past encounters by 5%.
  * *Mastery (Lvl 6, 25 Sneaks):* 35% chance to retain stealth without consuming stealth state upon success.
* **Backstab Track (Combat Ambush):**
  * Base Backstab: Automatic +20% damage multiplier from shadows or rear arc.
  * *Familiarity (Lvl 2, 10 Backstabs):* +10% additional damage multiplier (Total +30%).
  * *Mastery (Lvl 5, 25 Backstabs):* +25% additional damage multiplier (Total +45%).

---

### Cleric (Priest) Detailed Progression
* **Hit Die:** 1d8 (Max Con HP bonus +2).
* **Attack Bonus Rate:** +0.5 per level (+1 every 2 levels).
* **Spiritual Pool:** Divine Favor (100 Max Favor, increases by +5 per level).

#### Divine Favor Thresholds:
* **Full Communion (80–100 Favor):** Maximum prayer potency and complete connection to the deity.
* **Favorable Grace (40–79 Favor):** Standard casting efficacy.
* **Faint Connection (1–39 Favor):** Minor prayer disruptions possible.
* **Absolute Silence (0 Favor):** Prayers disabled until restorative rest or moral reconciliation.

#### Priest Skills & Powers:
* **Turn Undead:** Roll vs Undead Matrix (Skeleton, Zombie, Ghoul, Shadow, Wight, Wraith, Mummy, Spectre, Vampire). Base Target = `14 - Wisdom Modifier - (Level - 1)`.
* **Healing Insight:** Base score = `Wisdom + 3 + (Level - 1)`.
* **Prayers by Tier:**
  * Tier 1 (L1+): Cure Light Wounds, Bless, Sanctuary, Light, Protection from Evil.
  * Tier 2 (L3+): Hold Person, Spiritual Hammer, Silence 15' Radius, Aid.
  * Tier 3 (L6+): Prayer, Dispel Magic, Remove Curse, Cure Blindness.
  * Tier 4 (L9+): Cure Serious Wounds, Neutralize Poison, Protection from Evil 10'.

---

### Mage (Wizard) Detailed Progression
* **Hit Die:** 1d4 (Max Con HP bonus +2).
* **Attack Bonus Rate:** +0.34 per level (+1 every 3 levels).
* **Cognition Pool:** Vancian Cognition (100 Max Cognition base, +10 Cognition per level).
* **Arcane Spells by Tier:**
  * Tier 1 (L1+): Magic Missile, Shield, Sleep, Burning Hands, Shocking Grasp.
  * Tier 2 (L3+): Web, Invisibility, Mirror Image, Melf's Acid Arrow.
  * Tier 3 (L6+): Fireball, Lightning Bolt, Haste, Slow.
  * Tier 4 (L9+): Ice Storm, Stoneskin, Wall of Fire.

## 5. TRAINING, GUILD MENTORS & GOLD COSTS

Characters who accumulate enough XP do not instantly advance in the middle of a dungeon. To ascend to a higher level, the party must return to civilization (Town settlement, patron camp, or surface sanctuary) and pay the formal mentor training fee:

### Training Cost Formula
$$\text{Training Cost (gp)} = \text{Current Hero Level} \times 10\text{ gp}$$

* **Advancing to Level 2:** 10 Gold Pieces
* **Advancing to Level 3:** 20 Gold Pieces
* **Advancing to Level 4:** 30 Gold Pieces
* **Advancing to Level 5:** 40 Gold Pieces
* ...
* **Advancing to Level 10:** 90 Gold Pieces

### Official Mentor Sanctuaries
* **Fighter Training:** *Highstone Military Garrison* (Under Captain Valerius)
* **Thief Training:** *Thieves' Guild Crypt* (Under Master Jax Quick-Fingers)
* **Cleric Training:** *Sunfire Sanctuary* (Under High Priestess Kaelen)
* **Mage Training:** *Arcane Spire Sanctum* (Under Archmage Cynthia Ravenwing)
